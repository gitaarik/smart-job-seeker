# How Smart Job Seeker works

Smart Job Seeker turns a scattered job hunt into one organised pipeline. The
flow is simple:

1. **Build your profile once.** Your experience, skills, and preferences live in
   one place and power everything else.
2. **Scrape job boards.** SJS even suggests searches based on your profile when
   you import it; once configured, it opens them in a real browser and pulls in
   matching listings — automatically, on a schedule if you want.
3. **Match with AI.** Each job is scored against your profile so the best fits
   rise to the top instead of drowning in a feed.
4. **Apply and track.** Generate tailored cover letters and answers with AI, and
   keep every application, interview, and follow-up in one tracker.

## The on-device browser

The scraping step is what makes SJS different. Instead of a shared cloud bot
that job boards quickly detect and block, **SJS drives a real Chrome on a real
device with a real home IP address.** That's far more reliable — but it means a
device has to be running somewhere.

You have two options:

- **Run your own device** — a desktop app, or a small Docker container on a NAS
  or home server. See **[Devices & sharing](/guide/devices)**.
- **Use a device someone shared with you** — if a more technical friend invited
  you, you don't have to set up anything at all. Just pick their device when you
  run a search.

That's the whole idea: one person can host a device and bring in others who'd
never want to deal with the setup.
