# Usage & costs

Your plan comes with a monthly allowance of usage. It goes on the things that
cost us money to run: running a real browser on a job board, and asking a
language model to read or write something. Everything else in Smart Job Seeker
is free to use.

Nothing here is charged per seat or per application. The meter on
[Plan & Usage](/billing) shows how much of this month's allowance you have used,
and when it resets.

## Running out

When your plan's allowance is used up, you can add **extra usage** on the
[Plan & Usage](/billing) page, from €5. It is spent only once your monthly
allowance runs out, and whatever you don't use stays for next time: it doesn't
expire. If you find yourself adding extra usage every month, a bigger plan is
the cheaper way to get it.

## What uses your plan

The amounts below are what each thing costs when it comes out of extra usage.
On a plan it comes out of the monthly allowance instead, at the same
proportions, so they also tell you what is heavy and what is light.

**Running a job search.** A run is metered by what it actually did, not a flat
fee:

- 2 cents to start
- 1 cent for every job it brings in
- 1 cent for every 30 seconds of browser time
- 3 cents more if it had to log in for you

A run needs at least 15 cents of usage left before it starts, because a run that
dies halfway through is worse than one that doesn't begin. The final cost is
usually less than that.

**Matching.** Scoring a job against your profile is a small model call. Importing
and matching 100 jobs from your own device comes to about €8 all in.

**AI writing.** Cover letters, application answers, project stories, cheat
sheets, assistant messages, reviews and follow-ups are charged by how much text
the model actually read and wrote. A short question costs less than a long one,
and a cover letter written against a detailed job description costs more than
one against a two-line posting. A typical cover letter costs a few cents. Any of
them needs at least 5 cents of usage left to start.

**PDF export** costs 1 cent per document.

## What doesn't

Everything that isn't a browser run or a model call:

- Browsing jobs
- Editing your profile, your CV versions, and everything on them
- Tracking applications and logging activity
- Viewing and sharing documents in the browser
- Exporting your data, and importing it
- Applying a change the assistant suggested

Asking for Import Task **suggestions** is a model call and is charged like the
AI writing above, usually a single cent. Creating a task is free; running it is
the step that starts using your plan, because that's the step that runs a
browser.

## Using less

- **Tighten your Import Task filters.** A search that returns 200 jobs costs €2
  in job charges alone, and you will read maybe five of them. A narrow search is
  cheaper _and_ better.
- **Turn on community jobs** in [Match Config](/jobs/import/config). Scoring a
  job somebody else already imported costs a fraction of importing it yourself.
- **Give the AI a good profile.** The expensive part of a bad cover letter is
  regenerating it three times.
