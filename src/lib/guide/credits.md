# Credits

Credits are what the expensive things cost: running a real browser on a job
board, and asking a language model to write something. Everything else in Smart
Job Seeker is free to use.

Nothing here is charged per seat or per application. You spend on the two things
that cost us money, and you can see exactly where it went.

## What costs credits

**Running a job search.** A run is metered by what it actually did, not a flat
fee:

- 2 to start
- 1 for every job it brings in
- 1 for every 30 seconds of browser time — doubled if you're using a cloud
  browser rather than your own device
- 3 more if it had to log in for you

You need at least 15 credits available before a run will start, because a run
that dies halfway through is worse than one that doesn't begin. The final charge
is usually less than that.

**AI writing.** Cover letters, application answers, project stories, cheat
sheets, assistant messages, reviews and follow-ups are all charged the same way:
**1 credit per 10,000 tokens** the model actually processed — roughly, per 7,500
words in and out combined. A short question costs less than a long one; a cover
letter written against a detailed job description costs more than one against a
two-line posting. You need at least 5 credits available to start any of them.

**PDF export** costs 1 credit per document.

## What doesn't

Everything that isn't a browser run or a model call:

- Browsing jobs, and matching them against your profile
- Editing your profile, your CV versions, and everything on them
- Tracking applications and logging activity
- Viewing and sharing documents in the browser
- Exporting your data, and importing it
- Applying a change the assistant suggested

Import Task **suggestions** are generated free when you first import a profile.
Activating a task is the step that starts spending, because that's the step that
runs a browser.

## Spending less

- **Tighten your Import Task filters.** A search that returns 200 jobs costs 200
  credits in job charges alone, and you will read maybe five of them. A narrow
  search is cheaper _and_ better.
- **Use your own device rather than a cloud browser** — browser time is billed at
  half the rate.
- **Turn on community jobs** in [Match Config](/jobs/import/config). Scoring a
  job somebody else already imported costs a fraction of importing it yourself.
- **Give the AI a good profile.** The expensive part of a bad cover letter is
  regenerating it three times.
